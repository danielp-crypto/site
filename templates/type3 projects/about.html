About Type3 Projects

Powering safe, secure homes and businesses

For over 8 years, Type3 Projects has delivered reliable electrical, security, and automation solutions across Johannesburg and beyond—built on craftsmanship, trust, and responsive support.

Our story

Type3 Projects started with a simple mission: make professional electrical and security services accessible, honest, and dependable. From small call-outs to full installations, we approach every project with the same level of care and attention to detail.

Over the years, we’ve grown through referrals and long-term relationships with our clients. Our team has worked on homes, retail spaces, fuel stations, and industrial sites—always focusing on safety, neat workmanship, and clear communication.

Today, we combine solid on-site expertise with modern technology, helping you protect what matters most with CCTV, alarm systems, electric fencing, gate automation, and more.

People behind the work

Our technicians are trained, experienced, and passionate about solving real-world problems on site. We take pride in neat installations, clear labelling, and tidy handovers—so you always know how your system works and who to call if you need help.

Whether you’re upgrading security, automating access, or fixing electrical faults, you get direct access to the people who do the work—not a distant call centre.
